# Markets

The Markets tab delivers higher-level market structure and relative strength analysis.

---

### Market Structure Classification
Automatically labels the current regime:

- Trending Up
- Trending Down
- Ranging / Consolidation
- Volatile
- Transitioning

Also shows relative strength versus Bitcoin.

---

### Volume Profile
- Distribution of 24h volume across exchanges
- Identifies when a single exchange dominates (potential liquidity risk)
- Sentiment Score (0–100) combining buy/sell ratio, funding, and momentum

---

### Key Levels
- Consensus price
- 24h High / Low
- Total aggregated volume
- Price range positioning

This tab helps you understand the broader context and quality of the current move.
