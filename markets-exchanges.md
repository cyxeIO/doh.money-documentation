# Exchanges

The Exchanges tab gives you a detailed comparison of every venue listing the selected token.

---

### Volume Distribution
- Share of total trading volume by exchange
- Visual breakdown of dominance

### Bid / Ask Spread & Depth
- Spread ranking across exchanges
- Depth within 1% of mid price
- Identification of the tightest and deepest books

### Price Comparison Table
Real-time view of:
- Last price
- Best bid / ask
- Spread
- 24h performance
- Volume share

### Smart Signals
- Highlights when an exchange diverges significantly (potential arb window)
- Guidance on where larger orders should be routed for best execution

This tab is essential for understanding true liquidity quality and execution venue selection.
