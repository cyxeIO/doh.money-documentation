# Arbitrage Dashboard

Cross-exchange spread scanner and bot simulator.

### Features
- Scans **22 exchanges** in real time for live price discrepancies
- Net profit modelling after exchange fees, slippage, and latency
- Full bot simulator — configure capital, route, fees, and simulate execution

### Supported Exchanges
MEXC · Gate.io · Bybit · Binance · OKX · Bitget · Coinbase · KuCoin · HTX · Kraken · Gemini · Crypto.com · CoinEx · BitMart · Bitstamp · Bitfinex · Upbit · Phemex · LBank · XT.com · Pionex · Biconomy
