# Markets (Global Crypto Terminal)

A professional multi-exchange market intelligence platform.

### Core Features
- Real-time prices from major centralized exchanges
- Advanced TradingView charting
- Aggregated order book depth across multiple venues
- Bid/Ask pressure and slippage estimation
- Live trades tape
- Cross-exchange arbitrage monitoring
- Funding rates and liquidation data
- Fear & Greed index and market pulse
- News, social feeds, and alerts

The Markets terminal is designed for traders who need institutional-grade visibility across the entire crypto market.
