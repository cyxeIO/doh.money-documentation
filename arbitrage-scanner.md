# Spread Scanner

The Spread Scanner is the core real-time engine of the Arbitrage Dashboard.

It continuously compares prices across all supported exchanges and surfaces spreads that meet your criteria.

---

### Key Capabilities

- **Live Multi-Exchange Scanning**  
  Monitors hundreds of trading pairs across 22 exchanges simultaneously.

- **Net Profit Calculation**  
  Automatically factors in:
  - Trading fees on both sides
  - Estimated slippage
  - Latency / execution risk
  - Withdrawal fees (where relevant)

- **Smart Filtering**  
  Filter opportunities by:
  - Minimum net profit
  - Volume / liquidity
  - Specific exchanges
  - Tokens or categories

- **Opportunity Ranking**  
  Spreads are ranked by estimated profitability and execution quality.

- **Detailed Breakdown**  
  Click any opportunity to see the full calculation, route, and risk considerations.

---

### Best Practices

- Focus on opportunities with sufficient depth on both sides
- Account for deposit and withdrawal times when planning capital movement
- Use conservative slippage assumptions during volatile periods
- Monitor funding rates when including perpetual contracts

The Spread Scanner is designed to deliver clean, reliable signals so you can act with speed and clarity.
