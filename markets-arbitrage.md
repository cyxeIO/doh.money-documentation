# Arbitrage

The Arbitrage tab focuses on live cross-exchange price inefficiencies.

---

### Core Metrics

- **Gross Spread** — Raw price difference between highest bid and lowest ask
- **Net Spread** — Spread after estimated taker fees
- **Capital Required** — Approximate size needed to capture the opportunity
- **Profitable Pairs** count
- Best Buy and Best Sell exchanges

---

### Price Ladder
Ranked list of exchanges from lowest to highest price so you can instantly see the optimal route.

### Signals & Guidance
- Clear explanation of when a spread is actionable
- Warnings about withdrawal fees, transfer times, and slippage
- Pro tips for reliable arbitrage windows

All values update in real time as order books shift.
