# Arbitrage Dashboard

The Arbitrage Dashboard is a professional-grade cross-exchange spread scanner and strategy simulator built into DOH Money.

It continuously monitors price differences across **22 major centralized exchanges** and helps you identify, evaluate, and simulate profitable arbitrage opportunities in real time.

---

### Key Features

- **Real-time Scanning**  
  Continuously scans 22 exchanges for live price discrepancies across hundreds of trading pairs.

- **Net Profit Modelling**  
  Automatically calculates estimated net profit after accounting for:
  - Exchange trading fees
  - Slippage
  - Latency / execution risk
  - Withdrawal fees (where applicable)

- **Full Bot Simulator**  
  Test arbitrage strategies before risking real capital. Configure:
  - Starting capital
  - Trading route (Buy exchange → Sell exchange)
  - Fee structure
  - Slippage assumptions
  - Execution delay

- **Live Spread Visualization**  
  Clear display of current spreads, historical spread behavior, and opportunity frequency.

- **Opportunity Filtering**  
  Filter by minimum profit threshold, volume, specific exchanges, or tokens.

### How to Use the Arbitrage Dashboard

1. Open the **Arbitrage** section from the main navigation.
2. Browse the live list of detected spreads.
3. Click any opportunity to view detailed profit breakdown.
4. Use the **Bot Simulator** to model performance with your preferred parameters.
5. Adjust filters to focus on high-quality opportunities that match your risk tolerance and capital.

---

### Important Notes

- Arbitrage opportunities can appear and disappear within seconds. Speed of execution is critical.
- Always account for deposit/withdrawal times and potential temporary chain congestion when moving funds between exchanges.
- Past simulated results do not guarantee future profits.
- Start with small size when testing live execution.

---

The Arbitrage Dashboard is designed for traders who want a clear, data-driven edge in cross-exchange price inefficiencies — without needing to run multiple exchange terminals or build their own scanning infrastructure.
