# Alephium Dashboard

The **Alephium Dex Screener** is DOH Money’s native on-chain analytics terminal for the Alephium ecosystem. It delivers institutional-grade visibility into DEX activity, liquidity, token performance, holder behavior, capital flows, and individual wallet performance.

It is currently one of the most comprehensive analytics tools available for Alephium.

---

### Main Navigation Tabs

- **GLOBAL** — Ecosystem-wide overview
- **TRANSACTIONS** — Live and historical on-chain trades
- **TVL** — Total Value Locked analytics
- **LEADERS** — Top traders and volume leaders
- **HOLDERS** — Holder distribution
- **FLOW** — CEX inflows & outflows
- **TRACKER** — Advanced Wallet Tracker & Portfolio analysis

---

### GLOBAL Tab

Ecosystem intelligence at a glance:

- Total Volume and Netflow
- **Top 3 Performers** (Gold / Silver / Bronze cards) with Volume, Price Change, TVL, and Netflow
- Top Trading Pairs
- Top Performers & Top Decliners (24h)
- Volume Leaders
- Ecosystem Pressure (Buy vs Sell ratio)
- Whale Activity feed

---

### TRANSACTIONS Tab

Real-time and historical trade feed.

Features:
- Live trade stream with timestamp, wallet, pool, side (Buy/Sell), amounts, and price
- Expandable trade details
- Whale trade highlighting
- Click any wallet address to instantly open it in the Wallet Tracker
- Progressive loading of full trade history

---

### TVL Tab

Deep liquidity analytics for any pool:

- Current TVL (ALPH + USD)
- Long-term historical TVL chart (300+ days supported)
- All-time high and all-time change
- Pool stability and volatility metrics
- Breakdown of TVL changes (LP deposits/withdrawals vs price action)
- Recent LP events
- Full historical daily data table

---

### FLOW Tab (CEX Inflows & Outflows)

Tracks real-time movement of ALPH to and from centralized exchange hot wallets.

- Total inflows and outflows
- Breakdown by exchange (Gate.io, MEXC, Bitget, etc.)
- Daily inflow charts
- Top addresses sending to CEXs
- Multi-hop transaction tracing
- Ability to paste any address and trace its flow

---

### TRACKER Tab – Wallet Tracker & Portfolio Analytics

The Wallet Tracker is one of the most powerful features in the entire DOH Money platform. It provides Arkham-style wallet intelligence combined with full trading performance analysis.

#### How to Use
- Paste any Alephium wallet address
- Or click any wallet address from the Transactions tab

#### Core Wallet Overview
- Current Balance
- Total Inflow / Total Outflow / Net Flow
- Transaction count
- Wallet age
- Average transactions per week
- 30-day net flow

#### ALPH Movement Audit Report
- Full balance reconciliation (forward-walk verified)
- Peak balance and minimum balance
- Monthly ALPH movement breakdown
- Running net flow over time

#### Advanced Analytics

**Performance & Risk**
- Realized & Unrealized P&L
- Total ROI
- Win Rate
- Max Drawdown
- Profit Factor
- Average Win / Average Loss
- Risk-Reward ratio

**Portfolio & Holdings**
- Full token holdings with current value
- Cost basis tracking
- Per-token P&L and ROI
- Portfolio composition breakdown
- Known vs Unknown tokens

**Activity Intelligence**
- Activity Heatmap (26 weeks)
- Transaction Cadence analysis
- Dormancy periods detection
- Holding Period Analysis (FIFO)
- Transfer Size Distribution
- Largest individual transactions
- Counterparty analysis

**Behavioral Scoring**
- Wallet Score (Activity, Balance, Age, Diversity, CEX Usage)
- Sniper detection signals
- LP activity tracking

#### Export & Sharing
- Export to PDF
- Export to HTML
- Export to CSV / JSON
- Shareable Wallet Intelligence Report
