# Staking

Stake your **$DOH** tokens to earn a share of real protocol revenue.

### Revenue Sources
- Spot trading fees
- Launchpad fees
- Other protocol-generated revenue

### How to Stake
1. Go to the **Stake** tab
2. Enter the amount of DOH you wish to stake
3. Confirm the transaction

Staking is designed to be simple, transparent, and rewarding for long-term supporters of the ecosystem.
