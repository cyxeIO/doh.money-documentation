# Staking

DOH Staking allows you to stake $DOH tokens and earn a share of real protocol revenue generated from trading fees, launchpad activity, and other platform sources.

The staking dashboard is designed as a professional terminal experience with full transparency and real-time data.

---

### Key Metrics

- **Live APR** (variable, based on real revenue)
- **Total Value Locked (TVL)**
- **Number of Stakers**
- **Reward Pool status**
- **Your personal staked balance and claimable rewards**

---

### Core Features

- Stake and unstake $DOH at any time
- Real-time pending rewards tracking
- One-click claim
- Historical TVL and APR charts
- Top Stakers leaderboard
- Recent activity feed
- Reward calculator (estimate returns by amount and duration)
- Full wallet balance overview
- Network and contract information

---

### How Rewards Work

A portion of protocol revenue is directed to the staking reward pool and distributed proportionally to stakers. Rewards accrue continuously and can be claimed at any time.

---

### Tier Integration

Your staked $DOH balance determines your Launchpad tier, unlocking earlier access and higher allocation limits on token launches.

---

Staking is designed to align long-term holders with the success of the entire DOH ecosystem.
