# Risk Report

The Risk Report is a live, multi-dimensional risk assessment engine that evaluates tokens in real time using actual market data and trade flow.

It is designed to detect elevated risk and potential rug-pull conditions as they develop — not after the fact.

---

### How It Works

The system continuously analyzes **10 weighted metrics** and assigns both a letter grade (A+ to F) and a numerical score (0–100).

Metrics include:

- Price performance vs ATH/ATL
- Volatility history
- Supply distribution & circulating supply
- Exchange coverage and quality (Tier-1 vs lower tier)
- Order book health and depth
- Whale activity (buy vs sell pressure from large trades)
- Volume authenticity
- Funding rate health
- Market structure stability
- Real-time trade flow patterns

---

### Live Monitoring & Alerts

- Fully **real-time** — updates with every significant trade
- Detects abnormal selling pressure and potential dump signatures
- Surfaces early warning signals for rugs in progress
- Clear visual status (Safe / Low Risk / Moderate / High Risk)
- Detailed breakdown of every contributing factor
