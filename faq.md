# Frequently Asked Questions

**Is DOH.Money non-custodial?**  
Yes. You always remain in full control of your private keys.

**What networks are supported?**  
The DEX and core features are built on Alephium. The Markets terminal covers major global exchanges.

**Is there a mobile version?**  
Yes. The platform is fully responsive with a dedicated mobile experience.

**How can I reduce MEV risk on large trades?**  
Use the upcoming Protected Mode (Commit-Reveal) once it is live.

**Where can I get support?**  
Join the official DOH community channels.
