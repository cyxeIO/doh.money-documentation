# Overview

The Global Crypto Markets dashboard is a professional multi-exchange trading terminal.

### Key Features

* **Live Ticker Bar** — Real-time prices for 4200+ tokens + TradFi assets aggregated across 22 exchanges
* **NYSE Market Status** — Live open/closed/pre-market/after-hours badge with countdown + synthesized Opening Bell at 9:30 AM ET
* **Token List Tabs** — Hot · Gainers · Losers · Top · Arb · New · Favorites
* **Price Flash Animation** — Rows flash green/red on every tick
* **TradingView Advanced Chart** — Automatically selects the best exchange feed (Binance → Bybit → OKX → MEXC fallback)
* **Live Trades Feed** — Real-time buy/sell stream with whale detection
* **Order Book + Depth Chart** — Combined bid/ask ladders with cumulative depth visualization
* **Cross-Exchange Arbitrage** — Live spread scanner for every tracked token
* **Futures Data** — Funding rates, open interest, and funding arbitrage across Bybit, OKX, Gate, and Binance
* **Risk Report** — AI-style rug/dump risk scoring with live alerts
* **Journal** — Private per-session trading notes
* **News Feed** — Real-time financial news across multiple categories
* **Nostr Feed** — Decentralized crypto social timeline + per-token chatrooms
* **TV Tab** — CryptoWatch YouTube feed + full-screen TradingView chart
* **X/Twitter Feeds** — Multi-column or merged timeline

### Fully Customizable Sidebar

12 independently collapsible and drag-to-reorder sections: Live TV · Token Favorites · Rug Alerts · Token Details · Market Pulse · Futures · Fear & Greed · Top Movers · Latest News · X Feeds · Market Hours · Arbitrage
